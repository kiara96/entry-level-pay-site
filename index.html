How pay is spread

Share of salaried postings by monthly gross pay (€200 bands)

Internships here exclude work-study and working-student roles. They pile up at customary stipends (€500, €700–800, €1,000), with a second group near €2,000–3,000 where interns are paid as employees. Junior pay is tighter, centred on €2,000–3,000. Pay above €6,000 is grouped into the last band.

Median monthly pay, junior vs internship

Countries with at least 30 salaried junior postings; internship dot shown where there are at least 20 internships (work-study and working students excluded). The gap is widest in the Netherlands and Italy, where interns get an allowance. In the UK, Austria and Poland interns are paid close to junior rates: UK interns are usually employees, and Austrian ads quote collective-agreement pay.

Role and sector

Junior pay vs the country median, by function

Each posting is compared with the junior median in its own country, so a function is not rewarded for being common in a high-pay country. Consulting, software, sales, finance and research pay 13–21% above; hospitality pays about 8% below. Marketing, creative and legal are left out: once student roles are removed, fewer than 60 junior postings with pay remain.

Who pays the most for junior software, sales and operations roles

Dot: median pay gap against the junior median in each posting's own country, counting each employer once. Band: 80% bootstrap interval. Groups with at least 8 employers posting salaried junior roles in that function.

Industry is the employer's industry, not the role's: a developer at a supermarket counts under Commerce & Retail. The tech-industry sales premium and the startup sales premium partly share the same companies (12 of the 23 AI/software sales employers are startups). Sales pay ranges may include commission (on-target earnings), which can inflate startup sales figures. Climate & energy rests on 9–14 employers per function; read its wide bands as uncertainty.

Company

Monthly pay by company type

Median gross monthly pay. The line is the middle 80% of postings, the bar is the middle half, and the dot is the median. Junior pay is highest at startups and in the public sector and NGOs, and lowest at corporates, which publish most of the salaries. Internships sit near €800–€1,000, except in the public sector.
